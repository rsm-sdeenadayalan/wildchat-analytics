What it isA user-analytics product for teams shipping an AI assistant. It reads the logs and answers four questions: who, how often, for what, where it fails.
Why I built itEvery team has the logs. Almost none can read them without an engineer. This is the product a PM would build to fix that, end to end.
What's real3.2 million real conversations from a public dataset. Aggregates only, never transcripts. Every number is checked on every build.
